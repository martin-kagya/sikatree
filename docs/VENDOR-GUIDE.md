# Open your sikatree shop: a vendor guide

**Version 1.0 · 10 November 2026**

sikatree gives you one link with your photos, prices and orders. Customers open it, add what they
want to a cart, and check out with their name, phone and delivery address. You manage everything from
a simple dashboard.

Setting up takes about ten minutes. Most of that is choosing good photos.

---

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- **Your business name** — how customers will see it.
- **What you sell** — products, services, or both.
- **A shop link** — the part after `sikatree.africa/`, like `amasglow`. Lowercase letters, numbers and
  hyphens, 3 to 24 characters. It cannot be changed later, so pick one you like.
- **Your WhatsApp number** — in Ghana format, like `024 123 4567`.
- **An email you check** — this is how you sign in. There is no password.
- **Photos and prices** — three to five clear photos per item, the price in GH₵, and how many you have.
- **Delivery details** — what you charge and how long delivery takes, or whether customers pick up.

If you sell services, also decide the days and hours you take bookings.

## Create your shop

### Option A: From the website (no WhatsApp needed)

1. Open **`sikatree.africa/start`**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Business name**
   - **What do you sell?** — Products, Services, or Both
   - **Your shop link** — shown as `sikatree.africa/` followed by what you type
   - **WhatsApp number**
   - **Email**
   - Tick the box to agree to the terms and privacy notice
3. Tap **Create my shop**.
4. Open the email titled **“Your sikatree shop is live”** and tap the link inside. It works once and
   expires after 15 minutes.
5. You land on your dashboard. Your shop is live at `sikatree.africa/yourlink`.

If the link expires, go to **`sikatree.africa/auth/login`**, enter your email, and a fresh link will be
sent.

**Important:** your shop is live immediately, even before it has items. An empty shop says *“Nothing
here yet. This shop is still being set up.”* Add your items first, then share the link.

### Option B: From WhatsApp

Message the sikatree WhatsApp number with **START** and follow the prompts: agree to the terms, send
your business name, choose what you sell, and pick your shop link. You can add your first item right
there by sending a photo with a caption, or by typing a list like:

```
Vitamin C serum 120
Shea butter 45
```

Confirm the details, and the bot replies with your live shop link and a dashboard link. You can manage
everything else from the dashboard.

If you already created your shop on the website and later message the bot from the same number, it
recognises you and opens the menu instead of creating a second shop.

## Add your first items

From the dashboard, tap **Add item**.

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Photos** | Up to 5 photos (JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 10MB each, 30MB total). The first one is the cover. |
| **Type** | Only shown if you sell both products and services. Services are booked, not shipped. |
| **Name** | Keep it specific: “Vitamin C serum 30ml”, not “Serum”. |
| **Price (GH₵)** | The price customers pay. |
| **Compare-at price** | Optional. Shows the old price crossed out next to the new one. |
| **Description** | Up to 500 characters: what it is, what it does, size, ingredients, anything a buyer should know. |
| **Category** | A short label like “Serums” or “Cakes”. Suggestions come from the categories you set in Settings. |
| **Stock** | How many you have. Leave it blank for unlimited. Services do not use stock. |
| **Visibility** | **Active** (shown and buyable), **Sold out** (shown but not buyable), or **Hidden** (not shown at all). For services, the middle option reads **Unavailable**. |
| **Badge** | Optional: Discount, Popular or New. |
| **Position** | Lower numbers show first. Leave blank to add the item at the end. |

Tap **Add item**. You return to the dashboard with *“Item saved. Your page is up to date.”* and the item
is on your shop immediately.

### Good listing habits

- One clear photo on a plain background beats five blurry ones. Natural light, no heavy filters.
- Put the size or quantity in the name. Customers should not have to ask.
- Price everything. “DM for price” loses sales.
- Set stock honestly. At zero the shop shows **Sold out** automatically.
- Use **Hidden** for things you are still preparing, and **Sold out** for things you have run out of but
  want customers to see.

### Managing items later

On the dashboard, each item has:

- **Edit** — change the photo, price, description, stock, badge or position.
- **Sold out / Show** — take it out of sale or put it back, without deleting it.
- **Hidden** items stay on your list but are not on the shop.

**Photos:** uploading a new set replaces all the photos on that item. There is no way to delete just one
photo or reorder them yet; the first photo in the upload becomes the cover.

### Services

If you sell services, the item form hides stock and shows **Unavailable** instead of Sold out. Customers
pick a time from your availability instead of a delivery window. Set your availability before sharing —
without it, there are no bookable times.

## Set up the rest before you share

Open **Settings** from the dashboard.

### Categories

Write one category per line, in the order you want them shown:

```
Serums
Body care
Gift sets
```

This controls the category chips on your shop. An item whose category is not listed here still appears,
appended alphabetically.

### Delivery fee

Choose one:

- **Free delivery** — you charge nothing for delivery.
- **Fixed fee** — set one rate for your region and another for outside it. Pickup is always free.
- **Confirm after order** — the customer orders, then you quote the delivery fee. Use this when the
  price depends on the address.

In fixed mode, customers can also choose to pay the delivery fee in cash when the order arrives.

### Delivery time

Write what the customer sees after ordering:

- **Delivery time in your region** — e.g. “Delivery in 45 to 60 minutes”
- **Delivery time outside your region** — e.g. “Delivery in 1 to 2 days”
- **Pickup time** — e.g. “Ready for pickup in about 30 minutes”

Appointments use the date and time the customer chose, so these fields do not apply to them.

### Low-stock alerts

Set a number, e.g. `3`. When an order drops an item to that level or below, you get a WhatsApp alert.
Leave it blank to turn alerts off.

### Availability (services only)

Tick the days you work and set your hours, the length of each booking slot, and an optional weekly cap.
Add specific dates under **Fully booked or away** so customers cannot book them.

## Share your shop

At the top of the dashboard you will see:

- Your page link, e.g. `sikatree.africa/amasglow`
- **Copy link**
- **Copy share message** — copies: *“Shop with me on sikatree: sikatree.africa/amasglow”*
- **Share on WhatsApp**

Put the link in your WhatsApp status, your bio on Instagram or TikTok, your business cards, and your
replies when someone asks for prices. When a customer taps it, they see your items, categories, prices
and a cart.

Before you send it to a customer, open the link on your own phone and place a test order. That is the
fastest way to catch a wrong price or a missing delivery fee.

## What customers see

- Your shop name, location, categories and items.
- Item cards with photos, prices, badges and stock notes (“Only 3 left”, “Sold out”).
- A quick view with the full description and photo gallery.
- A cart and checkout: name, WhatsApp number, email, delivery or pickup, address, notes.
- For services: a date and time picker showing only your open slots.
- After ordering: an order code and a tracking page at `sikatree.africa/o/ORD-XXXX` showing status,
  items, total and delivery time, plus a reorder button.

---

## Orders

You get a WhatsApp alert for a new order, and it appears on your dashboard under **Orders**.

Each order shows:

- The order code (e.g. `ORD-9RTC`)
- What was ordered and the total
- How the customer is paying (pay on delivery, at pickup, at the appointment, or paid online)
- Customer name, phone and email
- Delivery address or appointment time
- Any notes the customer left

Move the order forward as you fulfil it:

1. **Confirm order** — you have seen it and accepted it.
2. **Mark ready** — ready for pickup or out for delivery.
3. **Mark completed** — delivered, picked up or the appointment is done.

If you cannot fulfil it, use **Cancel order** and add an optional reason. The customer sees the reason on
their tracking page and your stock is returned automatically. For bookings, the buttons read **Confirm
booking**, **Mark done** and **Cancel booking**.

You can also **Message** the customer on WhatsApp from the order, open the **Track page**, or copy the
tracking link.

### Online payments

If online payment is switched on, customers can choose **Pay now** and pay by Mobile Money or card
before the order is confirmed. You will see the order marked **Paid online**, and the order card
shows a **Paid online** badge.

An online order cannot be confirmed until the payment clears; until then it shows **Waiting for online
payment**. If the customer does not finish paying, the order cancels itself after about 30 minutes and
the stock is returned, so you never lose stock to abandoned checkouts.

If it is not switched on yet, every order is paid on delivery, at pickup or at the appointment.

## Signing back in

Go to **`sikatree.africa/auth/login`**, enter the email on your shop, and tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
The link works once and expires after 15 minutes. After you tap it you stay signed in for 30 days.

There is no password to remember and nothing to install.

- **Shops created on WhatsApp:** sign in by messaging the bot and choosing the dashboard option.
- **Lost the email?** Check spam, then request another link from the sign-in page.
- **Used or expired link?** Request a new one; each link works only once.

## Current limits

These are real today, so plan around them:

- **Your shop name, link and email cannot be changed from the dashboard yet.** Choose them carefully at
  signup, or contact support if you must change one.
- **There is no page editor yet** — no logo, bio, banner or theme picker. Your shop uses the name, items
  and categories you gave.
- **Up to 5 photos per item**, and re-uploading replaces all of them.
- **No variants** (sizes, colours) as separate options yet — put the size in the item name and price, or
  create a separate item.
- **No bulk import.** Items are added one at a time.
- **No payouts or refunds yet.** Online payments are still being tested; do not advertise **Pay now** to
  customers until we confirm it is live for you. Paid orders are recorded, and settlement follows separately.
- **No installable app.** The dashboard and shop work in the browser on a phone.
- **WhatsApp alerts depend on WhatsApp being connected.** If an alert does not arrive, the order is still
  on your dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Email sign-up is not configured yet” | New shops are temporarily closed. Try again later or contact `hello@sikatree.africa`. |
| The sign-in email never arrives | Check spam, confirm the email address, then request another link. Delivery can take a minute. |
| “That link is invalid, already used, or has expired” | Request a fresh link from the sign-in page. |
| “That link is already taken” | Choose another shop link. Someone has it, or it is reserved. |
| “That number already has a shop” | You already have a shop with that number. Sign in with your email, or with the bot if the shop was created on WhatsApp. |
| Your shop link says “Nothing here yet” | The shop is live but empty. Add your first item. |
| An item is missing from your shop | Check its **Visibility**: Hidden and Sold out items do not show as buyable. Also check stock is not zero. |
| A photo will not upload | Use JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF under 10MB each, no more than 5 per item, and 30MB total. |
| No WhatsApp alert for an order | Open your dashboard; the order is there. Report the missing alert if it keeps happening. |
| You cannot confirm an online order | It has not been paid yet. It will show **Waiting for online payment**, then cancel itself if the customer abandons it. |
| A customer cannot open your link | Send the exact link from **Copy link** on your dashboard. Check for a typo in the handle. |
